# Minato Maru lounge and passenger seating (12 November 2026)

Headless Chromium (SwiftShader) renders of `buildFerryModel()`, the same geometry the game draws. They are not the ink grade and not a physical-device capture. The sky is a plain colour because the ocean is not in this scene.

| File | View |
|---|---|
| `crew-lounge.png` | The bridge-deck crew lounge, from the corridor: dark carpet, tan leather settees, the wardroom table, the short bookshelf, the letter rack and a framed print. The private cabins are gone. |
| `passenger-seating.png` | Simple slatted benches on the old carpet-room platform, with a life-jacket locker between them. |

The lounge fittings follow the Chisum crew-lounge notes in `nj22az/nj22az.github.io` `ferry/yard/reference/owner-photographs-2026-11-11.md` (A1 and C5), and the benches follow the slatted bench in E7 of that note. They are redrawn in this town’s own boxes. The Chisum is not in the town, and that repository was not changed.
