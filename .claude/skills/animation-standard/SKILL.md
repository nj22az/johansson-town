# Animation standard

The owner, 27 November 2026: "https://github.com/dylantarre/animation-principles use this as the guidelines for animating
Johansson world make strict adherence to it".

1. Read `docs/ANIMATION-STANDARD.md` in full.
2. Read the twelve files `docs/animation-principles/skills/11-by-principle-focus/*/SKILL.md`, then the skills that the
   standard's section 1 names for the work in hand.
3. Put the move in its register (everyday or comedy, standard section 2) and write its brief (section 4, step 1) before
   any keys.
4. Make it to section 3's rules, then check it as section 4 says (the audit, the sheets with silhouettes, the game on two
   bodies), and report each principle with what was measured.

Never edit `docs/animation-principles/` (the guideline as the owner gave it) or an exported motion by hand.
